## Question-source policy

- Paper copies and question banks hosted by Scribd or Google Drive are **student-uploaded secondary sources**, not an official NU-hosted archive.
- A year is treated as a paper year only when it is visible in the paper copy or explicitly identified by the source.
- Questions transcribed from a readable paper are labelled separately from topics found only in a compiled question bank.
- Newly written revision questions are not described as previous-year questions.
- Verify the original paper scan before citing a question as an exact past-paper question.
