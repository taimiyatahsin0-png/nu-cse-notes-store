## 2. Essential definitions — Bangla first, then exam English

### 2.1 Magnetic Field

**বাংলা:** Magnet, current-carrying conductor বা moving charge-এর চারপাশে যে অঞ্চলে magnetic force অনুভূত হতে পারে, সেটিই magnetic field।

**English exam answer:** A magnetic field is a region around a magnet, current-carrying conductor or moving charge where magnetic forces can be experienced.

- Symbol: \(B\)
- SI unit: tesla (T)

### 2.2 Magnetic Flux Density

**বাংলা:** Magnetic field-এর strength বোঝাতে magnetic flux density \(B\) ব্যবহার করা হয়।

**English exam answer:** Magnetic flux density is the magnetic field quantity represented by \(B\), measured in tesla.

### 2.3 Magnetic Force on a Moving Charge

**বাংলা:** Magnetic field-এর মধ্যে moving charge থাকলে তার velocity এবং field-এর direction-এর ওপর নির্ভর করে force কাজ করতে পারে।

**English exam answer:** The magnetic force on a moving charge is the force exerted by a magnetic field on a charge in motion.

\[
\vec F=q(\vec v\times\vec B)
\]

\[
F=|q|vB\sin\theta
\]

### 2.4 Magnetic Dipole Moment of a Current Loop

**বাংলা:** Current loop-এর magnetic effect-এর magnitude বোঝায় magnetic dipole moment।

**English exam answer:** The magnetic dipole moment of a current loop is the product of the current, the area of the loop and the number of turns.

\[
\mu=NIA
\]

SI unit: \(A\,m^2\).

### 2.5 Torque on a Current Loop

**বাংলা:** Magnetic field-এর মধ্যে current loop-কে ঘোরানোর যে turning effect তৈরি হয়, সেটিই torque।

**English exam answer:** The torque on a current-carrying coil in a uniform magnetic field is the turning effect produced by the magnetic field.

\[
\tau=NIAB\sin\theta=\mu B\sin\theta
\]

### 2.6 Potential Energy of a Magnetic Dipole

**বাংলা:** Magnetic field-এর তুলনায় magnetic dipole কোন orientation-এ আছে, তার ওপর তার potential energy নির্ভর করে।

**English exam answer:** The potential energy of a magnetic dipole in a uniform magnetic field is the energy associated with its orientation relative to the field.

\[
U=-\vec\mu\cdot\vec B=-\mu B\cos\theta
\]

## 3. Formula sheet

| Quantity | Formula | SI unit |
|---|---|---|
| Force on moving charge | \(F=|q|vB\sin\theta\) | N |
| Radius of circular path | \(r=\frac{mv}{|q|B}\) | m |
| Angular frequency | \(\omega=\frac{|q|B}{m}\) | rad/s |
| Time period | \(T=\frac{2\pi m}{|q|B}\) | s |
| Force on straight wire | \(F=BIL\sin\theta\) | N |
| Force per unit length between parallel wires | \(\frac FL=\frac{\mu_0I_1I_2}{2\pi d}\) | N/m |
| Field near long straight wire | \(B=\frac{\mu_0I}{2\pi r}\) | T |
| Field at centre of N-turn circular coil | \(B=\frac{\mu_0NI}{2R}\) | T |
| Field inside ideal long solenoid | \(B=\mu_0nI\) | T |
| Magnetic dipole moment | \(\mu=NIA\) | \(A\,m^2\) |
| Torque on loop | \(\tau=\mu B\sin\theta\) | N m |
| Potential energy | \(U=-\mu B\cos\theta\) | J |

**Constants and symbols**

- \(\mu_0=4\pi\times10^{-7}\,T\,m/A\) (permeability of free space)
- \(q\): charge (C), \(v\): speed (m/s), \(m\): mass (kg)
- \(I\): current (A), \(L\): wire length (m), \(A\): coil area (\(m^2\))
- \(r\): distance or orbit radius (m), \(d\): separation (m)
- \(N\): number of turns, \(n=N/L\): turns per unit length

## 4. Important concepts and direction rules

### 4.1 Angle in magnetic-force formulas

For a moving charge, \(\theta\) is the angle between velocity \(\vec v\) and field \(\vec B\).

For a straight wire, \(\theta\) is the angle between the current direction and field \(\vec B\).

- Parallel directions: \(\sin0^\circ=0\), so force is zero.
- Perpendicular directions: \(\sin90^\circ=1\), so force is maximum.

For a negative charge, reverse the force direction obtained from the right-hand rule for a positive charge.

### 4.2 Torque and potential energy: do not mix up the angle

For a current loop, \(\theta\) is measured between the **area vector (normal to the plane of the loop)** and \(\vec B\).

| Angle | Torque | Potential energy | Equilibrium |
|---:|---|---|---|
| \(0^\circ\) | Zero | Minimum: \(-\mu B\) | Stable |
| \(90^\circ\) | Maximum: \(\mu B\) | Zero | Not an equilibrium |
| \(180^\circ\) | Zero | Maximum: \(+\mu B\) | Unstable |

**বাংলায় মনে রাখো:** Dipole field-এর দিকে align হতে চায়। তাই parallel অবস্থায় energy minimum, আর opposite অবস্থায় energy maximum।

### 4.3 Fleming's rules

**Left-hand rule — motor:** First finger = magnetic field, middle finger = conventional current, thumb = force or motion.

**Right-hand rule — generator:** First finger = magnetic field, thumb = conductor motion, middle finger = induced conventional current.

## 5. Solved numerical problems

### Problem 1: Force on a moving charge

**Question:** A charge of \(2\,\mu C\) moves at \(3\times10^4\,m/s\) perpendicular to a \(0.5\,T\) magnetic field. Find the magnetic force.

**Given:**

\[
q=2\times10^{-6}C,\quad v=3\times10^4m/s,\quad B=0.5T
\]

**Formula:**

\[
F=|q|vB\sin90^\circ
\]

**Substitution:**

\[
F=(2\times10^{-6})(3\times10^4)(0.5)
\]

\[
\boxed{F=0.03\,N}
\]

### Problem 2: Force on a current-carrying wire

**Question:** A \(0.4\,m\) wire carries \(5\,A\) current perpendicular to a \(0.2\,T\) field. Find the force.

\[
F=BIL\sin90^\circ
\]

\[
F=(0.2)(5)(0.4)=\boxed{0.4\,N}
\]

### Problem 3: Magnetic field due to a long straight wire

**Question:** Find the magnetic field at a distance of \(0.1\,m\) from a long straight wire carrying \(10\,A\).

\[
B=\frac{\mu_0I}{2\pi r}
\]

\[
B=\frac{(4\pi\times10^{-7})(10)}{2\pi(0.1)}
\]

\[
\boxed{B=2.0\times10^{-5}\,T}
\]

### Problem 4: Torque on a current loop

**Question:** A 20-turn coil has area \(0.01\,m^2\), current \(2\,A\), and magnetic field \(0.5\,T\). Find its maximum torque.

**Step 1 — Find magnetic dipole moment**

\[
\mu=NIA=20\times2\times0.01=0.4\,A\,m^2
\]

**Step 2 — Maximum torque occurs at \(90^\circ\)**

\[
\tau_{max}=\mu B=0.4\times0.5
\]

\[
\boxed{\tau_{max}=0.2\,N\,m}
\]

### Problem 5: Potential energy of a current loop

Using the same coil, find its potential energy when the angle between its area vector and the field is \(60^\circ\).

\[
U=-\mu B\cos60^\circ
\]

\[
U=-(0.4)(0.5)(0.5)
\]

\[
\boxed{U=-0.1\,J}
\]

### Problem 6: Work done in rotating a magnetic dipole

Find the external work required to rotate the same loop slowly from \(0^\circ\) to \(60^\circ\).

At \(0^\circ\):

\[
U_0=-\mu B=-(0.4)(0.5)=-0.2\,J
\]

At \(60^\circ\):

\[
U_{60^\circ}=-0.1\,J
\]

For slow rotation without change in kinetic energy:

\[
W_{ext}=\Delta U=U_{60^\circ}-U_0
\]

\[
W_{ext}=-0.1-(-0.2)=\boxed{0.1\,J}
\]

## 6. Short questions and answers (2 marks)

**Q1. What is the SI unit of magnetic flux density?**  
Answer: Tesla (T).

**Q2. When is the magnetic force on a moving charge zero?**  
Answer: When the charge moves parallel or antiparallel to the magnetic field, or when it is at rest.

**Q3. Does a magnetic field change the speed of a charged particle?**  
Answer: A magnetic force is perpendicular to the particle's velocity and does no work; therefore, its speed remains constant.

**Q4. What is the direction of force between two parallel wires carrying currents in the same direction?**  
Answer: They attract each other.

**Q5. State the field inside an ideal long solenoid.**  
Answer: \(B=\mu_0nI\).

**Q6. What is the magnetic dipole moment of a coil?**  
Answer: \(\mu=NIA\).

**Q7. At what angle is the torque on a current loop maximum?**  
Answer: At \(90^\circ\), measured between the area vector and the magnetic field.

**Q8. At what orientation is the potential energy of a magnetic dipole minimum?**  
Answer: When \(\vec\mu\) is parallel to \(\vec B\), at \(\theta=0^\circ\).

## 8. Common mistakes to avoid

- Use the absolute value of charge when calculating force magnitude; use charge sign when finding direction.
- Check whether the angle is between velocity/current and magnetic field.
- Do not use the angle between the coil's plane and the field in \(\tau=\mu B\sin\theta\); use the area-vector angle.
- Do not say that a magnetic force changes the speed of a charged particle in a purely magnetic field.
- Convert microcoulomb to coulomb: \(1\,\mu C=10^{-6}C\).
- Write units in every final numerical answer.
