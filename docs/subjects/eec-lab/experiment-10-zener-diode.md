## 1. Objective
To study and experimentally verify experiment 10: study of zener diode v–i characteristics using instructor-approved apparatus and measured observations.

A Zener diode is designed to operate in reverse breakdown within its rated current and power limits. In the breakdown region, its voltage remains approximately VZ over a range of reverse current. A series resistor is essential to limit current. Power dissipation is PZ = VZIZ.

## 7. Calculations
Calculate PZ = VZ × IZ. Plot reverse current against Zener voltage and identify the approximate regulation region. Never exceed the datasheet maximum power or current.

## 8. Result and discussion
In breakdown, voltage changes relatively little while current changes more. Series resistance, temperature and device tolerance affect regulation.

### Short answers
A Zener regulator uses reverse breakdown of a suitably rated Zener diode. The series resistor limits current; the diode's power rating must not be exceeded.
