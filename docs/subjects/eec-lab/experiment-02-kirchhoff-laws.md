## 2. Theory (সহজ বাংলা ধারণা)
Kirchhoff’s Current Law (KCL): the algebraic sum of currents at a node is zero; total current entering equals total current leaving. Kirchhoff’s Voltage Law (KVL): the algebraic sum of voltage rises and drops around a closed loop is zero. These laws follow from conservation of charge and energy.

## 8. Result and discussion
Small residuals are expected because of resistor tolerance, meter loading, lead resistance and reading uncertainty. A large mismatch often indicates incorrect polarity, wiring or meter connection.

## 9. Precautions

- Check polarity, meter mode, range and component ratings before switching on.
- Use current limiting and begin with the lowest practical source setting.
- Never connect an ammeter in parallel with a source; never short an unknown source.
- Record actual readings with units; do not fabricate observation values.

### Short answers
