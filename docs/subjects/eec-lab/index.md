
# Electrical and Electronic Circuit Lab

**Course Code:** 510204  
**Credits:** 1.5  
**Marks:** 40  
**Semester:** First Year – First Semester  
**Related Theory Course:** 510203 – Electrical and Electronic Circuit

## Course Objectives

This laboratory course is based on Electrical and Electronic Circuit (510203).

Students will develop practical skills in electrical circuit analysis, electronic components and experimental verification of circuit laws and theorems.

## Official Laboratory Experiments

### Experiment 1: Ohm's Law
- Verification of Ohm's Law
- Measurement of Resistivity of a Metallic Wire

### Experiment 2: Kirchhoff's Laws
- Verification of Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL)
- Verification of Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (KVL)

### Experiment 3: Thevenin's Theorem
- Experimental Verification of Thevenin's Theorem

### Experiment 4: Norton's Theorem
- Experimental Verification of Norton's Theorem

### Experiment 5: Superposition Theorem
- Experimental Verification of Superposition Theorem

### Experiment 6: RC Circuit
- Study of an RC Circuit
- Determination of Time Constant

### Experiment 7: RLC Series Resonance
- Study of an RLC Series Resonance Circuit

### Experiment 8: Diode Characteristics
- Study of the I-V Characteristics of a Diode

### Experiment 9: BJT Characteristics
- Input and Output Characteristics of BJT
- Common-Base (CB) Configuration
- Common-Emitter (CE) Configuration

### Experiment 10: Zener Diode
- Study of I-V Characteristics of a Zener Diode
