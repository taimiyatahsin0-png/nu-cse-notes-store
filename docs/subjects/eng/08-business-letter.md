# Business Letter Writing

## Core rules and method
**Standard format:**
Sender's address / contact
Date
Receiver's name/designation and address
Subject
Salutation (Dear Sir/Madam,)
Opening purpose
Relevant details in logical order
Requested action / closing sentence
Yours faithfully / sincerely,
Name and designation

## Examples / Practice with answers
**Sample: Request for Information**

Subject: Request for Information About Course Registration

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing to request information about the course registration process for the current semester. I would be grateful if you could inform me of the registration deadline, required documents and applicable fees.

I would appreciate your guidance at your earliest convenience.

Yours faithfully,
A Student

## Exam tips
**Language:** I am writing to…; I would be grateful if…; Please let me know…; Thank you for your assistance.

**Avoid:** slang, emojis, vague subject lines, emotional accusations and unnecessary personal details.
