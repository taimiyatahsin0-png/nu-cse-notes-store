## 1. Reading Comprehension

### সহজ বাংলায়
Reading comprehension মানে passage পড়ে তার মূল বক্তব্য, নির্দিষ্ট তথ্য, শব্দের অর্থ এবং implied meaning বুঝে প্রশ্নের উত্তর দেওয়া। Passage-এর বাইরে নিজের মতামত যোগ করবে না।

### Step-by-step method
1. Title ও passage দ্রুত পড়ে topic বোঝো।
2. প্রশ্নগুলো পড়ে keyword চিহ্নিত করো।
3. Passage-এ keyword বা তার synonym খুঁজে বের করো।
4. সংশ্লিষ্ট বাক্যের আগে-পরে পড়ে context বোঝো।
5. প্রশ্ন যতটুকু চায়, ততটুকুই উত্তর দাও।
6. Inference-এর ক্ষেত্রে passage থেকে প্রমাণ পাওয়া যায় কি না যাচাই করো।

### Original practice passage
Technology has changed the way students learn. Online libraries, educational videos and digital classrooms allow learners to access information from many places. However, having access to information does not automatically mean that a student understands it. Students need to compare sources, identify reliable information and practise what they learn. They should also take breaks and maintain a balance between screen time and other activities. Technology becomes most useful when learners use it thoughtfully and responsibly.

### Questions
1. What is the main idea of the passage?
2. Mention two ways technology supports learning.
3. Why does access to information not guarantee understanding?
4. What should students do before accepting online information?
5. Suggest a suitable title for the passage.

### Model answers
1. The passage explains how students can use technology thoughtfully and responsibly for learning.
2. It provides access to online libraries and educational videos. It also supports digital classrooms.
3. Because students must understand, compare and practise the information they receive.
4. They should compare sources and check whether the information is reliable.
5. **Responsible Use of Technology in Learning.**

**Question types:** Main idea = central message; specific information = directly stated fact; inference = reasonable conclusion supported by the passage; vocabulary in context = meaning in that sentence; True/False = every detail must agree with the passage.

## 2. Articles: A, An and The

### সহজ বাংলায়
Article noun-এর আগে বসে। A/An সাধারণত অনির্দিষ্ট singular countable noun-এর আগে বসে; The নির্দিষ্ট বা পরিচিত noun বোঝায়। Article নির্বাচন spelling নয়, প্রথম sound অনুযায়ী হবে।

### Rules

| Article | Use | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| A | Consonant sound-এর আগে | a book, a university, a European country |
| An | Vowel sound-এর আগে | an apple, an hour, an honest person |
| The | নির্দিষ্ট বা আগে উল্লেখ করা noun | the book on the table |
| The | Unique object / superlative | the sun, the best student |
| No article | General plural or uncountable noun | Books are useful. Water is essential. |

**Sound rules:** a university (starts with /y/ sound); an hour (silent h); a one-eyed man (one begins with /w/ sound). General names such as Bangladesh, Barishal and English normally take no article.

### Practice: Fill in the blanks
1. She is ___ honest student.
2. I saw ___ European tourist.
3. ___ earth moves around ___ sun.
4. He bought ___ umbrella.
5. My brother wants to become ___ engineer.
6. We study at ___ university.
7. ___ information you gave me was useful.
8. She is ___ first person to arrive.
9. He waited for ___ hour.
10. ___ students should respect their teachers.

### Answer key
1. an
2. a
3. The, the
4. an
5. an
6. a
7. The
8. the
9. an
10. No article

## 3. Correct Form of Verbs

### সহজ বাংলায়
Verb-এর সঠিক form নির্ভর করে subject, tense, auxiliary verb, voice এবং sentence structure-এর ওপর। Blank পূরণের আগে subject ও time expression চিহ্নিত করবে।

### Core rules

| Rule | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Present simple | V1 / V1+s(es) | She works. |
| Past simple | V2 | They went. |
| Present continuous | am/is/are + V-ing | He is reading. |
| Present perfect | has/have + V3 | She has written. |
| Past perfect | had + V3 | They had left. |
| Modal | modal + V1 | You should study. |
| Passive | be + V3 | The letter was written. |
| Gerund | verb + ing | He enjoys reading. |
| Infinitive | to + V1 | She wants to learn. |

**Special reminders**
- Did-এর পরে base verb: did not go, not did not went.
- Can, should, must-এর পরে V1: can speak.
- Has/have/had-এর পরে perfect construction-এ V3।
- Preposition-এর পরে verb এলে সাধারণত gerund: interested in learning.
- If I were you একটি standard hypothetical expression.
- Since starting point বোঝায়; for duration বোঝায়.

### Practice: Use the correct form
1. She usually (go) to college by bus.
2. They (complete) the assignment already.
3. The letter (write) yesterday.
4. He enjoys (read) novels.
5. You should (follow) the instructions.
6. By the time we arrived, the train (leave).
7. If I (be) you, I would accept the offer.
8. The students (prepare) for the examination now.
9. He has been living here (since/for) 2022.
10. Neither of the answers (be) correct.

### Answer key
1. goes
2. have completed
3. was written
4. reading
5. follow
6. had left
7. were
8. are preparing
9. since
10. is

**Why #10 is is:** In formal exam grammar, neither is treated as singular here, so it takes a singular verb.

## 4. Conjunctions

### সহজ বাংলায়
Conjunction এমন word যা word, phrase বা clause যুক্ত করে। Blank-এর আগে-পরে meaning দেখে বুঝবে addition, contrast, reason, result, choice নাকি condition বোঝানো হয়েছে।

### Main types
- **Coordinating:** for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so.
- **Subordinating:** because, although, if, when, while, unless, before, after.
- **Correlative:** either...or, neither...nor, both...and, not only...but also.

| Conjunction | Meaning / use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| because | কারণ | I stayed home because I was ill. |
| although | যদিও | Although it rained, we played. |
| but | কিন্তু | He is poor, but honest. |
| so | তাই / ফলাফল | It was late, so we left. |
| unless | যদি না | You cannot pass unless you study. |
| while | যখন / contrast | She read while I wrote. |
| either...or | দুটির একটি | Either Rafi or Sami will lead. |
| neither...nor | কোনোটিই নয় | Neither tea nor coffee is available. |

### Practice
1. I stayed at home ___ I was ill.
2. ___ he is young, he is very responsible.
3. Work hard ___ you can achieve your goal.
4. You cannot enter ___ you have a valid ticket.
5. ___ Rafi or Sami will present the report.
6. She is not only intelligent ___ hardworking.
7. I wanted to call you, ___ my phone battery was dead.
8. He was reading ___ his sister was cooking.

### Answer key
1. because
2. Although
3. so that
4. unless
5. Either
6. but also
7. but
8. while

**Common error:** Although it was raining, but we continued is incorrect. Write either “Although it was raining, we continued” or “It was raining, but we continued”.

## 5. Correct Sentences

### সহজ বাংলায়
Sentence correction-এ ভুল grammar, tense, word order, article, preposition, number, pronoun বা parallel structure ঠিক করতে হয়। প্রথমে subject ও main verb চিহ্নিত করো। অপ্রয়োজনে পুরো sentence নতুন করে লিখবে না।

### Common rules
1. **Subject–verb agreement:** She does not know me.
2. **Preposition:** He is good at English.
3. **Comparison:** She is senior to me.
4. **Preference:** I prefer tea to coffee.
5. **One of + plural noun:** One of my friends is a doctor.
6. **No sooner...than:** No sooner had he arrived than it started raining.
7. **Double negative:** I do not know anything.
8. **Parallel structure:** She likes reading, writing and speaking.

### Practice: Correct the sentences
1. He do not like coffee.
2. She is senior than me.
3. I prefer tea than coffee.
4. One of my friend lives in Barishal.
5. He is good in mathematics.
6. No sooner he reached the station when the train left.
7. Each of the students have a notebook.
8. I am looking forward to meet you.

### Answer key with reasons
1. **He does not like coffee.** — He takes does.
2. **She is senior to me.** — Senior takes to.
3. **I prefer tea to coffee.** — Prefer A to B.
4. **One of my friends lives in Barishal.** — One of + plural noun.
5. **He is good at mathematics.** — Correct collocation: good at.
6. **No sooner had he reached the station than the train left.** — Inversion with had; use no sooner...than.
7. **Each of the students has a notebook.** — Each is singular.
8. **I am looking forward to meeting you.** — Here to is a preposition, so use a gerund.

## 6. Paragraph Writing

### সহজ বাংলায়
Paragraph-এ একটি central topic থাকবে। প্রথম sentence-এ topic introduce করবে, মাঝের sentence-গুলোতে explanation ও example দেবে, শেষে concluding sentence লিখবে।

### Structure
1. **Topic sentence:** মূল বিষয় পরিচয় করায়।
2. **Supporting sentences:** কারণ, ব্যাখ্যা ও উদাহরণ দেয়।
3. **Concluding sentence:** মূল বক্তব্য সুন্দরভাবে শেষ করে।

### Writing process
Brainstorm → arrange ideas → write a topic sentence → add 3–5 relevant supporting sentences → conclude → revise grammar and spelling.

### Sample paragraph: Importance of Time Management
Time management is an essential skill for students. It helps them use their limited time wisely and complete academic tasks on schedule. A student can prepare a daily routine, set priorities and divide large assignments into smaller tasks. Regular revision also becomes easier when study time is planned. Good time management reduces unnecessary stress and creates space for rest and personal development. Therefore, every student should develop the habit of managing time effectively.

### More topics to practise
- The Importance of Education
- The Uses and Abuses of the Internet
- Climate Change
- The Value of Discipline
- A Good Citizen
- The Importance of Learning English

**Useful connectors:** Addition — moreover, furthermore; example — for instance; contrast — however, on the other hand; result — therefore, as a result; sequence — first, next, finally.

**Exam check:** Keep one central idea, use relevant supporting points, maintain consistent tense and avoid repeating the same idea.

## 7. Summary Writing

### সহজ বাংলায়
Summary হলো passage-এর central idea ও essential supporting points নিজের ভাষায় সংক্ষেপে লেখা। Example, repetition, minor details এবং personal opinion বাদ দেবে।

### Method
1. Passage দুইবার পড়ো।
2. Main idea ও essential points চিহ্নিত করো।
3. Example ও repeated information বাদ দাও।
4. নিজের ভাষায় একটি coherent paragraph লেখো।
5. প্রশ্নে word limit থাকলে সেটি মেনে চলো।
6. Original passage-এর meaning পরিবর্তন করো না।

### Practice passage
Trees are essential to human life and the natural environment. They absorb carbon dioxide and release oxygen, helping to maintain the balance of gases in the atmosphere. Their roots hold soil together and reduce erosion. Trees also provide food and shelter for many animals. In cities, they offer shade and help reduce heat. However, deforestation destroys habitats and creates serious environmental problems. Protecting forests and planting trees are therefore necessary for a healthier planet.

### Model summary
Trees support environmental balance by absorbing carbon dioxide, releasing oxygen, protecting soil and providing habitats for wildlife. They also reduce heat in urban areas. Therefore, preventing deforestation and planting trees are important for a healthy planet.

**Why this is a summary:** It keeps the central message and major supporting ideas while removing repeated or less important details.

## 8. Business Letter

### সহজ বাংলায়
Business letter হলো formal communication। ভাষা হবে polite, clear, specific এবং professional। Subject line দেখে recipient যেন চিঠির উদ্দেশ্য বুঝতে পারেন।

### Standard format
1. Sender's address
2. Date
3. Receiver's name/designation and address
4. Subject
5. Salutation
6. Opening purpose
7. Relevant details
8. Requested action / closing sentence
9. Complimentary close
10. Name and designation

### Sample: Request for Information
**Subject: Request for Information About Course Registration**

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing to request information about the course registration process for the current semester. I would be grateful if you could inform me of the registration deadline, required documents and applicable fees.

I would appreciate your guidance at your earliest convenience.

Yours faithfully,  
A Student

**Useful expressions:** I am writing to enquire about...; I would be grateful if you could...; I would appreciate it if you would...; Please let me know...; Thank you for your assistance.

**Remember:** Use a clear subject, formal salutation and polite closing. Do not use slang, emojis or unnecessary personal details.

## 9. Complaint Letter

### সহজ বাংলায়
Complaint letter-এ কোনো product বা service-এর সমস্যা facts দিয়ে ব্যাখ্যা করবে। শুধু অভিযোগ করাই লক্ষ্য নয়; replacement, repair, refund বা অন্য reasonable solution চাইবে।

### Structure
Sender details → Date → Receiver details → Subject → Salutation → Purchase/service details → Problem → Evidence/reference → Requested solution → Closing.

### Sample: Complaint About a Defective Product
**Subject: Complaint Regarding a Defective Electric Kettle**

Dear Sir/Madam,

I purchased an electric kettle from your store on 5 October 2026 (Receipt No. 12345). Unfortunately, the kettle stops working after a few minutes of use, although it has been used according to the instructions.

I request you to inspect the product and arrange a replacement or refund under the applicable warranty. A copy of the receipt is available for verification.

I would appreciate a response within seven working days.

Yours faithfully,  
A Customer

**Useful expressions:** I wish to bring to your attention...; Unfortunately, the product/service...; I request that you...; I would appreciate a prompt resolution.

**Remember:** State dates and receipt/order numbers accurately. Avoid insults, threats and unsupported claims.

## 10. Final Revision Checklist

- [ ] I can identify the main idea and supporting details in a passage.
- [ ] I can choose a, an, the or no article.
- [ ] I can identify tense, subject agreement, voice and verb pattern.
- [ ] I can choose conjunctions according to meaning.
- [ ] I can correct common grammar and sentence-structure errors.
- [ ] I can write a paragraph with a topic sentence and conclusion.
- [ ] I can write a concise summary without adding personal opinion.
- [ ] I can follow the formal business-letter format.
- [ ] I can write a polite, fact-based complaint letter.

## Exam-writing reminders

- Read the instruction and word limit before writing.
- Keep handwriting and paragraph spacing clear.
- Do not invent facts in comprehension answers.
- Check subject–verb agreement, tense, articles and punctuation.
- In letters, keep the purpose and requested action explicit.
- Leave a little time to proofread.
