# Complaint Letter Writing

## সহজ বাংলায় ধারণা
Complaint letter-এ কোনো পণ্য, সেবা বা ঘটনার সমস্যা ভদ্র কিন্তু দৃঢ়ভাবে জানানো হয়। লক্ষ্য হলো সমস্যা সমাধান: replacement, repair, refund, correction বা written response চাওয়া।

## Core rules and method
**Structure:** sender details → date → receiver details → subject → salutation → purchase/service details → factual description of the problem → evidence/reference number → reasonable requested solution → response deadline if appropriate → formal closing.

## Examples / Practice with answers
**Sample: Complaint About a Defective Product**

Subject: Complaint Regarding a Defective Electric Kettle

Dear Sir/Madam,

I purchased an electric kettle from your store on 5 October 2026 (Receipt No. 12345). Unfortunately, the kettle stops working after a few minutes of use, although it has been used according to the instructions.

I request you to inspect the product and arrange a replacement or refund under the applicable warranty. A copy of the receipt is available for verification.

I would appreciate a response within seven working days.

Yours faithfully,
A Customer

## Exam tips
**Useful expressions:** I wish to bring to your attention…; Unfortunately…; I request that you…; I would appreciate a prompt resolution.

**Avoid:** insults, threats, unsupported claims or exaggeration. State dates, order/receipt numbers and facts accurately.
