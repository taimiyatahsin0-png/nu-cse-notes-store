# Summary Writing

## সহজ বাংলায় ধারণা
Summary হলো passage-এর মূল বক্তব্য সংক্ষেপে নিজের ভাষায় প্রকাশ করা। উদাহরণ, পুনরাবৃত্তি, অপ্রয়োজনীয় বর্ণনা বাদ যাবে; কিন্তু লেখকের central meaning বদলানো যাবে না।

## Core rules and method
1. Read the passage twice.
2. Identify the central idea and essential supporting points.
3. Underline keywords; remove examples, repetition and minor details.
4. Write in your own words, usually in one coherent paragraph.
5. Keep the required word limit if the question gives one.
6. Do not add personal opinion or information absent from the passage.

## Examples / Practice with answers
**Original idea:** Trees provide oxygen, absorb carbon dioxide, protect soil, support wildlife and make surroundings cooler. Deforestation harms ecosystems and increases environmental problems.

**Possible summary:** Trees are essential to environmental balance because they support life, protect soil and help regulate the climate. Therefore, deforestation creates serious ecological problems.

## Exam tips
**Remember:** summary is shorter than the original; preserve the main idea; avoid direct copying; do not add a title unless requested; use correct grammar and logical linking.
