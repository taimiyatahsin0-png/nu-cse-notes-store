# Correct Sentences and Common Errors

## Core rules and method
**Agreement:** She does not like tea. (not She do not…)

**Tense consistency:** He went home and slept. (avoid unnecessary tense shifts)

**Word order:** She always arrives early.

**Pronoun:** Each student must bring his or her/their ID (follow the required style).

**Parallelism:** She likes reading, writing and speaking.

**Comparative:** better than; senior to; different from.

**Double negative:** I do not know anything. (not I do not know nothing.)

## Examples / Practice with answers
1. He do not know me. → He does not know me.
2. She is senior than me. → She is senior to me.
3. I prefer tea than coffee. → I prefer tea to coffee.
4. One of my friend is a doctor. → One of my friends is a doctor.
5. He is good in English. → He is good at English.
6. No sooner he arrived when it rained. → No sooner had he arrived than it rained.

## Exam tips
**Exam checklist:** subject–verb agreement; tense; article; preposition; singular/plural; pronoun reference; parallel structure; punctuation; spelling. Change only what is necessary; do not rewrite a correct sentence unnecessarily.
