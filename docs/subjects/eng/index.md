


# English

**Course Code:** 510209  
**Course Title:** English  
**Credits:** 3.0  
**Marks:** 80  
**Class Hours:** 45  
**Semester:** First Year – First Semester

## Course Overview

This course adopts an integrative approach to developing the four basic language skills: Speaking, Listening, Reading and Writing.

Special emphasis is given to the development of reading and writing skills.

**Assessment Note:** 30% of the total marks will be allotted to class work in reading and writing.

# Syllabus Topics (Reference: Session 2017–2018)

## 1. Speaking Skills

- Strategies for Effective Communication
- Introduction to Phonetics
- Effective Oral Presentation
- Making Statements
- Making Requests
- Making Inquiries
- Disagreeing
- Complaining and Apologizing
- Discussion and Other Oral Presentations

## 2. Listening Skills

- Listening to Spoken English
- Understanding Spoken Communication
- Taking Useful Notes

## 3. Reading Skills

- Reading Literary Essays
- Reading General Essays
- Reading Comprehension
- Understanding Literary Communication
- Developing Reading Strategies

## 4. Grammar in Use

Grammar will generally be taught in context.

The following grammatical aspects may be taught:

- Articles
- Verb Patterns
- Sentence Combining
- Subordination and Coordination
- Conditional Sentences
- Infinitives
- Gerunds
- Participles
- Subject-Verb Agreement

## 5. Writing Skills

- Paragraph Writing
- Précis Writing
- Analytical Writing
- Writing on Current Affairs
- Scientific Writing

## 6. Commercial Correspondence

- Defining Context
- Feedback
- Semantic Gap
- Different Types of Commercial Letters
- Business Letter Writing
- Tender-Notice Writing
- Pre-Qualification Notice Writing
- Report Writing on Specific Topics
