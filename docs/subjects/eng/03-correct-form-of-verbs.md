# Correct Form of Verbs

## Core rules and method
**Subject–verb agreement:** He works; They work.

**Present perfect:** has/have + past participle — She has written.

**Past perfect:** had + past participle — They had left before I arrived.

**Modal:** modal + base verb — can go, should study.

**Passive:** be + past participle — The letter was written.

**Gerund:** enjoy/avoid/finish + verb-ing — She enjoys reading.

**Infinitive:** want/decide/hope + to + base verb — He wants to learn.

**Since/for:** present perfect often pairs with since (starting point) and for (duration).

## Examples / Practice with answers
1. He (go) to college every day. — goes
2. They (finish) the work already. — have finished
3. The letter (write) yesterday. — was written
4. She enjoys (read). — reading
5. You should (respect) your teachers. — respect
6. By the time we arrived, the train (leave). — had left
7. If I (be) you, I would apologize. — were

## Exam tips
**Frequent traps:** modal + V1; did + V1; has/have/had + V3; passive = be + V3; preposition + gerund (interested in learning). Check subject agreement after phrases such as each of, everyone and neither.
