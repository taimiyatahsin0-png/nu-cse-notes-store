# Reading Comprehension

## Core rules and method
1. Read the title and first/last sentences to identify the topic.
2. Read the questions and underline keywords.
3. Scan the passage for matching names, dates, ideas or synonyms.
4. Read the relevant sentence plus the surrounding sentence.
5. Answer exactly what is asked, using your own words where possible.
6. For inference questions, distinguish what the passage supports from personal opinion.

## Examples / Practice with answers
**Main idea:** What is the central message of the passage?

**Specific information:** Find a stated fact; do not add outside knowledge.

**Inference:** Choose a conclusion logically supported by the text.

**Vocabulary in context:** Replace the word with a possible synonym and check whether the sentence meaning remains logical.

**True/False:** Every part of the statement must agree with the passage. If one detail conflicts, it is False.

## Exam tips
**Mini passage:** Regular exercise improves physical fitness and can support mental well-being. Even a short daily walk may help people build a healthy routine. However, exercise should be appropriate for a person's age and physical condition.

1. What is the passage mainly about? — Benefits of regular exercise.
2. Mention one possible benefit. — It improves physical fitness / supports mental well-being.
3. Why should exercise be appropriate? — Because age and physical condition differ.
