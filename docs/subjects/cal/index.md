
# Calculus

**Course Code:** 510205  
**Course Title:** Calculus  
**Credits:** 3.0  
**Marks:** 80  
**Class Hours:** 45  
**Semester:** First Year – First Semester

## Course Overview

This course covers the fundamental concepts of Differential Calculus and Integral Calculus, including functions, limits, continuity, differentiation, integration and their applications.

# Official Syllabus Topics

## Part A: Differential Calculus

### 1. Functions and Their Graphs
- Polynomial Functions
- Rational Functions
- Logarithmic Functions
- Exponential Functions
- Trigonometric Functions and Their Inverses
- Hyperbolic Functions and Their Inverses
- Combinations of Functions

### 2. Limits of Functions
- Definition of Limits
- Basic Limit Theorems
- Limits at Infinity
- Infinite Limits

### 3. Continuity
- Continuous Functions
- Algebra of Continuous Functions
- Properties of Continuous Functions on Closed and Bounded Intervals

### 4. Differentiation
- Tangent Lines and Rates of Change
- Definition of Derivative
- One-sided Derivatives
- Rules of Differentiation
- Successive Differentiation
- Leibnitz Theorem
- Related Rates
- Linear Approximations
- Differentials

### 5. Mean Value Theorems
- Rolle's Theorem
- Lagrange's Mean Value Theorem
- Cauchy's Mean Value Theorem

### 6. Applications of Derivatives
- Extrema of Functions
- Maxima and Minima
- Concavity
- Points of Inflection

### 7. Taylor's Theorem and Indeterminate Forms
- Taylor's Theorem
- Taylor Series
- Indeterminate Forms
- L'Hospital's Rule

## Part B: Integral Calculus

### 8. Integration
- Antiderivatives
- Indefinite Integrals
- Techniques of Integration
- Definite Integration Using Antiderivatives
- Definite Integration Using Riemann Sums

### 9. Fundamental Theorems and Properties
- Fundamental Theorems of Calculus
- Basic Properties of Integration
- Integration by Reduction

### 10. Applications of Integration
- Plane Areas
- Solids of Revolution
- Volumes by Cylindrical Shells
- Volumes by Cross-sections
- Arc Length
- Surface of Revolution

### 11. Advanced Integration Topics
- Improper Integrals
- Gamma Functions
- Beta Functions

### 12. Polar Coordinates
- Graphing in Polar Coordinates
- Tangents to Polar Curves
- Area in Polar Coordinates
- Length in Polar Coordinates
