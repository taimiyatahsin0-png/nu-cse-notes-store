# Mathematical Symbols & Notation for Beginners

**Course:** Calculus (510205)  
**Level:** Beginner  
**Purpose:** Learn what mathematical signs mean, how to read them aloud, and how they are used in Calculus.

> **Study tip:** Do not try to memorize every symbol at once. Learn a small group, read it aloud, and solve a few examples.

---

## 1. How to read a mathematical expression

A mathematical expression is like a sentence. Symbols are its words.

Example:

`2 + 3 = 5`

- `2` — two
- `+` — plus
- `3` — three
- `=` — equals
- `5` — five

Read it in English: **“Two plus three equals five.”**  
বাংলায়: **“দুই যোগ তিন সমান পাঁচ।”**

## 3. Greater than and less than

These signs compare two values.

| Symbol | Meaning | Read in English | বাংলা |
|---|---|---|---|
| (>) | Greater than | is greater than | বড় |
| (<) | Less than | is less than | ছোট |
| (geq) | Greater than or equal to | is greater than or equal to | বড় অথবা সমান |
| (leq) | Less than or equal to | is less than or equal to | ছোট অথবা সমান |

**Remember:** The open/wide side faces the larger number.

- (7 > 3): Seven is greater than three.
- (2 < 9): Two is less than nine.
- (x geq 4): x is greater than or equal to four.

## 4. Fractions, powers and roots

### Fraction

(rac{a}{b}) means **a divided by b**. Here, (a) is the numerator (উপরের সংখ্যা) and (b) is the denominator (নিচের সংখ্যা). The denominator cannot be zero.

Example: (rac{3}{4})  
Read: “Three over four” or “three divided by four.”

- (x

## 7. Intervals

Intervals describe a range of real numbers.

| Notation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ((2,5)) | (2 < x < 5), endpoints excluded |
| ([2,5]) | (2 leq x leq 5), endpoints included |
| ([2,5)) | (2 leq x < 5) |
| ((2,5]) | (2 < x leq 5) |
| ((-infty,3)) | All real x less than 3 |

**Memory trick:** A round bracket excludes its endpoint; a square bracket includes its endpoint. Infinity always uses a round bracket.

## 8. Function notation

(f(x)) is read **“f of x”**. It means the value of the function (f) when the input is (x). It does not mean (f) multiplied by (x).

Example: If (f(x)=x+2), then:

- (f(3)=3+2=5)
- Read (f(3)): “f of three.”

Other common forms:

| Notation | Read | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| (f(x)) | f of x | Function value |
| (f(a)) | f of a | Function value at a |
| (f(g(x))) | f of g of x | Composite function |
| (y=f(x)) | y equals f of x | y depends on x |

## 9. Limit notation — especially important

The symbol (lim) means **limit**. It asks what value a function approaches as its input gets close to a particular number.

[
lim_{x	o 2}(x+3)=5
]

Read in English: “The limit as x approaches two of x plus three equals five.”

বাংলায়: “x যখন ২-এর কাছাকাছি যায়, তখন x plus ৩-এর মান ৫-এর কাছাকাছি যায়।”

| Symbol | Meaning | How to read |
|---|---|---|
| (lim) | Limit | limit |
| (x	o 2) | x approaches 2 | x tends to two |
| (x	o a) | x approaches a | x tends to a |
| (x	o 0^+) | x approaches zero from the right | x tends to zero from the positive side |
| (x	o 0^-) | x approaches zero from the left | x tends to zero from the negative side |
| (infty) | Infinity | infinity |
| (-infty) | Negative infinity | negative infinity |
| (0/0) | Zero divided by zero | zero over zero; an indeterminate form in limits |

**Important:** (x	o 2) does not mean x becomes exactly 2. It means x gets closer and closer to 2.

### One-sided limits

- (x	o a^-): x approaches a from values smaller than a (left-hand side).
- (x	o a^+): x approaches a from values greater than a (right-hand side).

## 16. A simple 10-minute daily learning routine

- **2 minutes:** Read five symbols aloud.
- **3 minutes:** Write each symbol and its Bangla meaning.
- **3 minutes:** Read two complete mathematical expressions.
- **2 minutes:** Solve two very easy examples using those symbols.

Repeat this routine until the notation feels familiar. Understanding the symbols first will make limits, continuity and differentiation much easier.
